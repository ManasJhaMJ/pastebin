# BinPaste

**The better pastebin alternative.** A free, fast way to share **code snippets** and **text** online — no account required.

🔗 **Live:** https://www.binpaste.xyz/

## What is BinPaste?

BinPaste lets you paste any text or code, give it a custom name, and instantly get a shareable link like `www.binpaste.xyz/<your-name>`. Send the link to anyone — friends, teammates, or a support thread — and they can view, copy, or download it. No sign-up, no clutter.

## Features

- **Custom paste names** — pick your own name, or generate a random unused one in one click. Your paste lives at `www.binpaste.xyz/<your-name>`.
- **Syntax highlighting** — Plain Text, JavaScript, Python, Java, C, C++, HTML, and CSS.
- **Expiring pastes** — optionally have a paste expire after 10 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, or 1 month (default: never).
- **Public pastes feed** — opt-in to showcase a paste on the public page.
- **One-click copy** — copy the paste content instantly; the share link is auto-copied when you create a paste.
- **Download & raw view** — download a paste as a file, or open its plain-text raw view.
- **QR codes** — every paste gets a scannable QR code for quick sharing to a phone.
- **Rich link previews** — shared links show a clean preview card on social apps and chat.
- **No account required** — just open the site and paste.

## How It Works

1. Type or paste your text, pick a language, and choose a custom name (or generate one).
2. Optionally set an expiry time and mark the paste public.
3. Create the paste — the shareable link is copied to your clipboard automatically.
4. Share the link. Anyone can view, copy, download, or open the raw text — no account needed.
